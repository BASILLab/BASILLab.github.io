Meet the Team

Principal Investigator

Kyeonggu Lee, Ph.D.

Department of Biomedical Engineering
Pusan National University

Prof. Kyeonggu Lee received his B.S. (2019) in Biomedical Engineering and his M.S. (2021) and Ph.D. (2024) in Electronic Engineering from Hanyang University, Seoul. He completed his graduate studies under the supervision of Prof. Chang-Hwan Im. Following his doctorate, he conducted postdoctoral research at Hanyang University (Sep. 2024 – Feb. 2026) and as a postdoctoral researcher at CHU Sainte-Justine & Université de Montréal (Mar. – Sep. 2026), under the supervision of Prof. Karim Jerbi and Prof. Anne Gallagher.

He is now an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at Pusan National University, where he leads BASIL Lab. His research spans deep learning for biomedical signals (EEG, fNIRS), brain-computer interfaces (BCI), AI-driven clinical diagnostics, and cognitive neuroscience.

// 02. Research

Core Research Areas

EEG Foundation Models

Adaptation and Generalization of EEG Foundation Models

Developing methods to adapt pretrained EEG models across participants, recording configurations, and tasks for brain–computer interfaces and clinical applications.

Explainable AI

Explainable AI for Biomedical Signal Analysis

Applying explainable machine learning to EEG and fNIRS data to identify disease-related signal features and support interpretable computer-aided diagnosis.

Cognitive Neuroscience

Machine Learning for Cognitive Neuroscience

Using machine learning to characterize neural activity associated with cognition, brain development, and cognitive decline across the lifespan.

Neuromodulation

Personalized and Closed-Loop Neuromodulation

Using physiological signals and treatment-response prediction to guide personalized stimulation and develop closed-loop neuromodulation approaches.
