Welcome to BASIL Lab

Advancing Biomedical Signal Intelligence

At BASIL Lab (Biomedical AI for SIgnaLs Laboratory), we harness the power of Artificial Intelligence to decode and understand complex biomedical signals — from brain waves (EEG, fNIRS, MEG) to physiological biosignals — transforming raw neural data into clinical insights for early disease diagnosis and next-generation Brain-Computer Interfaces.

// 01. About Me

Bridging AI & Computational Neuroscience

Advancing translational neuroengineering, brain-computer interfaces, and clinical neurodiagnostics.

Hello & Welcome! 🧠

I am a researcher affiliated with Université de Montréal, dedicated to pioneering intelligent technologies that bridge human neurophysiology and modern artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on developing novel deep learning architectures for neuroimaging and biosignals (fNIRS, EEG, MEG), optimizing wearable neurotechnology for early disease diagnosis (such as Mild Cognitive Impairment and Major Depressive Disorder), and engineering robust Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) systems.

Key Research Directions

  • Brain-Computer Interfaces & Wearable EEG

    Developing SSVEP-based BCI and optimal electrode configurations for scalable wearable cognitive screening.

  • Deep Learning & Neurodiagnostics (CADx)

    Designing custom CNN channel-embedding layers and meta-classifiers for Major Depressive Disorder diagnosis.

  • Photobiomodulation & Neuromodulation

    Predicting patient responsiveness to transcranial near-infrared light (tPBM) therapy using concurrent fNIRS data.

// 02. Research Focus

Core Research Domains

Interdisciplinary exploration uniting computational modeling, neuroscience, and psychiatric healthcare.

Artificial Intelligence

Designing specialized deep learning architectures (CNNs with channel-embedding layers), meta-classification ensembles, and Explainable AI (XAI / SHAP) tailored for high-dimensional biomedical time-series and neuroimaging data.

Deep Learning Channel Embedding Explainable AI Meta-Classification

Brain-Computer Interface

Engineering high-accuracy SSVEP-based BCIs, multimodal systems combining electrooculography (EOG) and augmented reality (AR) for assistive smart homes, and optimal electrode channel reduction for wearable devices.

SSVEP BCI Wearable EEG AR & Eye Tracking Assistive Tech

Psychology & Neuropsychiatry

Translating neural biomarkers into objective Computer-Aided Diagnosis (CADx) tools for Major Depressive Disorder (MDD), Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI), dementia early warning systems, and affective state decoding.

Depression (MDD) MCI & Dementia Cognitive Decline Biomarker Discovery

Computational Neuroscience

Investigating prefrontal cortical hemodynamics using fNIRS, cortical source localization with MEG (MEGIN TRIUX neo), and predictive machine learning models for transcranial photobiomodulation (tPBM) neuromodulation.

fNIRS Hemodynamics MEG Source Imaging Photobiomodulation (tPBM) Signal Processing
